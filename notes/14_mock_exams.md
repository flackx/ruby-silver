# 14. Mock exams

The two mock exams under `mock_exams/` each contain 50 questions and use the official time limit of 90 minutes.

## Conditions

- Do not run Ruby or use documentation.
- Use one uninterrupted 90-minute session.
- Record one or two choices exactly as requested.
- Mark uncertain answers even when guessing correctly.
- Do not open `mock_exams/answers/` until the session ends.

## Scoring

Award one point only when the complete selection is correct. A choose-two response with one correct and one incorrect choice earns zero.

- Below 70%: return to the relevant notes and topic quizzes.
- 70–79%: near the official threshold but not booking-ready.
- 80–84%: promising; remove repeated weak areas.
- 85% or higher: readiness range, provided the score is repeated on another unseen mock.

After marking, classify every miss as one of: missing fact, wrong rule, tracing error, question-reading error, or time pressure. Add it to the mistake log and schedule a closed-book recheck.

## Common traps

- Do not memorize option letters; explain the rule.
- A remembered official-sample question is not evidence of readiness.
- Warnings, syntax errors, and runtime exceptions are different answers.
- A high untimed score does not replace a timed mock.
